Journal

How the paintings come about, what happens at the fairs, the work of the studio. Written in Orléans, France.

  • 15 October 2026 Atelier Joconde: a father, his two sons and three signatures in Orléans Atelier Joconde, in Orléans, France: my father Le Yack, my brother Neo Pablo and me, Popsan. Three painters in one family, a gallery and art fairs.
  • 14 October 2026 art3f Monaco: my Popsan and Medsan wall, three sales and Chou’s Spine’s departure From 11 to 13 September 2026, at the art3f fair in Monaco, my wall mixed Popsan and Medsan. Akari, Kingyo and the kintsugi wave sold; Chou’s Spine left too.
  • 13 October 2026 art3f Luxembourg: my Popsan wall on the Atelier Joconde stand From 2 to 4 October 2026, at the art3f fair in Luxembourg, I had a Popsan wall on the Atelier Joconde stand, with my father, Le Yack, and my brother, Neo Pablo.
  • 13 October 2026 Chou’s Spine at “La Vie en Rose”: broken heart syndrome on show in Paris My Medsan painting Chou’s Spine, about broken heart syndrome, was shown at “La Vie en Rose” at the Hôtel de l’Industrie in Paris on 26 September 2026.
  • 12 October 2026 Painting on a black background: how Akari steps out of the night Akari starts from a completely black ground: the skin is built up in glazes and the lantern comes last. Sold in Monaco, a tribute to Georges de La Tour.
  • 12 October 2026 Kintsugi: what the art of golden repair means, and my wave with golden cracks Kintsugi repairs broken ceramics by tracing the cracks in gold. What it means, and my kintsugi Great Wave off Kanagawa, shown in Monaco.
