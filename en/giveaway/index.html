10K giveaway: the rules

To celebrate 10,000 followers on @medsan.art, an artwork to win.

Article 1 — Organiser

The giveaway is run by William San (William Pelletier), a painter based in Orléans, France, on his Instagram account @medsan.art. Contact: medsan.artist@gmail.com.

Article 2 — Dates

From Monday 2 November 2026, when the giveaway post is published, to Sunday 15 November 2026 at 11:59 pm (Paris time).

Article 3 — Eligibility

Entry is free and no purchase is necessary. It is open worldwide to anyone aged 18 or over, except the organiser and the members of Atelier Joconde.

Article 4 — How to enter

  1. Follow @medsan.art.
  2. Like the giveaway post.
  3. Comment on the post with the number of the artwork you want (1, 2 or 3) and tag a friend.

Each valid comment counts as one entry. To be valid, a comment must tag a different friend, using a real account (no brand accounts, fake accounts or accounts created for the occasion).

Optional bonuses:

  • repost the post with Instagram’s “Repost” button: all your entries count double;
  • repost the post AND share it to your story, tagging @medsan.art: all your entries count triple.

A private account must stay visible to @medsan.art for its story to count; a screenshot sent by direct message during the giveaway is also accepted.

Article 5 — Prize

One winner receives a fine art print on canvas, signed and numbered by hand by the artist, 40 × 40 cm, of the artwork whose number appears in their winning comment:

  1. Imari Cortex;
  2. Aqua Mentis;
  3. Chou’s Spine.

Retail value: €300 including VAT (public price on med-san.art). Free shipping worldwide. The prize cannot be exchanged or redeemed for cash.

Article 6 — Draw

On Monday 16 November 2026, a random draw picks the winner among all valid entries, bonuses included. The winner is announced in a story and in a reply to their comment. They are contacted only by direct message from @medsan.art: the organiser never asks for a payment or bank details. The winner has 7 days to reply with a delivery address; without a reply, a new draw takes place.

Article 7 — Fraud

Any entry obtained through fake, automated or purchased accounts, or by any means contrary to Instagram’s rules, is void.

Article 8 — Personal data

The details sent by the winner (name, address) are used only to ship the prize and are deleted after delivery.

Article 9 — Instagram

This giveaway is in no way sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram or Meta. Every entrant releases Instagram from any liability.

Article 10 — Governing law

These rules are governed by French law. They can be read at williamsan.art/giveaway.

William San

Painter, Orléans
